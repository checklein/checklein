## Becoming a maintainer

Checklein aims to be neutral infrastructure, so maintainership is meant to spread across organizations. Contributors with a record of reviewed, merged work (adapters, conformance vectors, integrations, security fixes) can be nominated by any maintainer. The project plans to move to a neutral foundation once maintainers from at least two outside organizations are active.
